# Study data

- `cie1931_2deg_xyz_1nm.csv` — CIE 1931 2-degree XYZ colour-matching functions, 360-830 nm at 1 nm, from CVRL
  (http://www.cvrl.org/database/data/cmfs/ciexyz31_1.csv), columns: wavelength nm, x, y, z.
- `Au_johnson_christy_um_n_k.txt`, `Cu_johnson_christy_um_n_k.txt` — P. B. Johnson and R. W. Christy, Optical constants of
  the noble metals, Phys. Rev. B 6, 4370 (1972), via refractiveindex.info (CC0); columns: wavelength um, n, k.
- `Ag_johnson_christy_um_n_k.txt` — Johnson & Christy 1972 (silver); `Fe_johnson_christy_um_n_k.txt` — P. B. Johnson and
  R. W. Christy, Optical constants of transition metals, Phys. Rev. B 9, 5056 (1974); `Al_rakic_um_n_k.txt` — A. D. Rakić,
  Appl. Opt. 34, 4755 (1995). All via refractiveindex.info (CC0).
